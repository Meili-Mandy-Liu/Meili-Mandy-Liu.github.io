About Meili

I am an Assistant Scientist at the University of Miami working at the intersection of molecular modeling, materials science, and data-driven discovery.

My research integrates molecular dynamics, density functional theory, machine learning, and multiscale computational approaches to understand molecular-scale mechanisms and guide the design of sustainable and functional materials.

My current research interests include computational materials science, molecular simulation, sustainable materials, interfacial chemistry, and AI/ML-assisted materials discovery.

Research Interests

  • Molecular Modeling & Simulation
  • Sustainable & Functional Materials
  • Interfacial Chemistry
  • AI/ML for Materials Discovery
  • Multiscale Materials Science

Research Environment

M³D Research leverages interdisciplinary collaborations, advanced molecular simulation, machine learning, and high-performance computing resources to connect molecular-scale understanding with materials design.

Our research draws on shared computational and experimental facilities at the University of Miami and collaborating institutions.
